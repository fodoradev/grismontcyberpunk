## What Grismont is

An independent process and technology practice run by Attila. It uses Lean Six Sigma to simplify how work gets done, then builds automation and AI only where they actually help. It covers the whole path, from the first "why does this take so long?" to a solution the team can use and keep running.

## The core idea

**Understand the work before you buy the tool.**

Most teams reach for software or AI to fix a process nobody has looked at closely. Grismont reverses the order:

1. **Find the friction** (Lean): follow the work and see where it waits, repeats or loses information.
2. **Make it reliable** (Six Sigma): agree on a consistent process and measure what matters.
3. **Automate the routine** (Automation): connect the steps that follow clear rules, with a way to handle exceptions.
4. **Add AI with purpose** (AI): use it where interpretation helps, with clear data rules and human checks.

> "The right answer can be a better checklist. It can also be a custom tool. We find out before we build."

This order is built into the product too. The scorecard won't rate a process ready for automation or AI if its basics (stability, data) aren't there yet.

## Who it's for

Teams and leaders who feel that some task, handover or report takes more effort than it should, and who are wondering about automation or AI but don't yet know where to start. They don't need to know Lean Six Sigma. Work is in Hungarian or English, on-site in Hungary or online elsewhere.

## The entry point: the free Discovery Scorecard

The main call to action across the site.

- **What:** 30 concrete questions about one process you know well.
- **Time:** about 7–8 minutes. Free, no obligation to book anything.
- **Six areas:** Flow and waste · Stability and standards · Data and measurement · Automation potential · AI readiness · People and ownership.
- **You get:** six scores with plain-language explanations, three suggested quick wins, the main risk to address before automation or AI, and a practical 90-day roadmap. Shown on screen straight away, plus a PDF by email.
- **Next step:** reply to the report email for a 30-minute review.
- **Tagline:** "Before the next tool, a clearer picture." / "Six areas. One process. A clearer next step."

There is also a deeper **Advanced Audit** (44 questions, about 8 minutes, resumable) with a detailed assessment and a prioritised 90-day improvement plan. It is not publicly indexed yet, so don't promote it until it is.

## Voice and tone

- **Calm, practical, human.** Short sentences. Plain words. No hype, no buzzwords, no "revolutionise" or "10x".
- **Curious rather than salesy.** Lead with a question or a recognisable situation, not a pitch.
- **Respectful of the team.** They already know the work; Grismont helps it flow.
- **Honest about AI.** It's a tool for specific tasks, with data rules and human checks, not a magic fix.
- Signature devices: a short headline in two beats ("First, understand it. Then, improve it."), the arrow `↳` for "here's the follow-through", and before → after flows.

## Content pillars for Instagram

1. **Spot the tangle.** Everyday process pain people recognise (weekly report, approval loop, re-keyed data, chasing emails). Hook: "Recognise this?"
2. **Before → after.** Carousel showing a tangled flow and a clearer one. The site's three examples are ready-made:
   - *The weekly report:* Export → Copy & paste → Check again → Send **becomes** One agreed source → Automatic update → Review exceptions.
   - *The approval loop:* Request → Forward → Chase → Forward again **becomes** Clear request → Named decision maker → Decision & record.
   - *The AI idea:* Broad idea → Choose a tool → Try prompts → Unclear result **becomes** Defined task → Test with safeguards → Measure & decide.
3. **Method in plain words.** One post per step (Lean, Six Sigma, Automation, AI), each with a single practical tip.
4. **AI, sensibly.** Myth vs. reality; "Could AI do this?" is the start of the question, not the answer; what has to be true before AI helps.
5. **The question to ask.** One sharp diagnostic question per post, e.g. "Which numbers do people actually use to make a decision?", "Who can say yes, and what do they need to know?", "What would a useful answer look like, and who checks it?"
6. **Six areas, one at a time.** A six-part series walking through the scorecard areas, each ending with "Where does your process stand? Free scorecard, link in bio."
7. **Behind the work.** Attila's perspective: the busy-Tuesday reality, the "why" habit, lessons from operations.

## Guardrails

- **Don't invent results.** No client names, percentages, hours saved or testimonials exist on the site yet. Use illustrative examples, labelled as such, until real case studies are approved.
- **Credentials stay exact:** Lean Six Sigma *Green Belt*. Don't upgrade it.
- **Leave bracketed placeholders alone.** Anything like `[Free / price]` in the copy is unresolved business information and must not be filled in on social.
- **Booking:** there's no booking link yet; point to the scorecard or email.
- **Privacy-first:** the site uses no analytics, cookies or tracking. That's worth mentioning, and it rules out promising things like "personalised follow-up based on your browsing".
- **Bilingual:** the site is in English and Hungarian. Plan posts in both or decide on a primary language per account.
