## 1. Positioning: one sharp focus that both audiences recognise

### The problem to solve
- Your first clients will probably be manufacturing and logistics companies.
- Your deepest experience is office work.
- You want one sharp message, but office teams must also feel that it is for them.

### The solution: focus on the *office side of operations*
Every manufacturing and logistics company has a large office layer around the shop floor:
order entry, production planning, purchasing, shipping documents, quality reports, invoicing,
shift handovers, approvals and weekly reports. This layer is usually the slowest and least
improved part of the company. Lean consultants focus on the shop floor. IT companies focus on
software. Few people focus on the work *between* them.

That is your focus:

> **I improve the office work around operations — orders, reports, approvals and handovers —
> and train teams to keep improving it.**

Why this works for both readers:
- **A manufacturing or logistics reader** sees "orders, planning, shipping documents, shift reports".
  It is their world. You look specialised.
- **An office or services reader** sees "reports, approvals, handovers, copy-paste between systems".
  It is also their world. The problems are the same in any company.
- **You do not claim shop-floor expertise you cannot prove yet.** You claim the area where your
  experience is deepest, inside the industry where your first clients are.

### Positioning statement (internal, not for the page)
For manufacturing and logistics companies with 20–250 people, Grismont improves the office
processes that connect orders to delivery. Unlike shop-floor Lean consultants or software vendors,
Grismont starts with the process, trains the team in practical Lean, and automates or adds AI only
where the numbers justify it.

### How to write the copy so both groups feel relevant
1. Name the industry once, clearly, in the hero sub-line ("for manufacturing, logistics and the
   office teams behind them").
2. Then describe *problems*, not industries: "the weekly report takes a day", "the order waits for
   three approvals", "the same data is typed into two systems".
3. In each example, give one manufacturing detail and keep the rest universal.
   Example: "The weekly production report" — a factory reader sees production; an office reader
   sees "weekly report".
4. In the FAQ, answer "We are not a factory. Is this for us?" with a clear yes.

## 2. Offer ladder: training first

Training is your best first product:
- It has a fixed scope and price, so the buyer's risk is low.
- HR/L&D budgets exist for training, also in companies with no "consulting" budget.
- **Each training creates proof.** Build an applied improvement task into every training. Each
  participant team improves one real process and measures the result within 30 days. You get a
  measured result (case study material) from every training.
- It leads to delivery work: teams find problems that need help to fix.

| Step | Offer | Format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **Discovery Scorecard** | Free, online, 7–8 min | Lead capture; first value |
| 1 | **Folyamat-feltáró workshop / Process discovery workshop** | ½ day, on-site or online, 1 process | Low-risk entry; finds the savings; fee credited to step 2 or 3 |
| 2 | **Tréning, ami megtérül / Training that pays for itself** | 1-day team training + applied task + 30-day results review (online, 1 hour) | Main product. Topics: Lean in the office; Problem solving (A3/8D light); AI at work |
| 3 | **Fejlesztési program / Improvement programme** | 3 training days over 6–8 weeks + team improvement projects + results report | Larger, repeatable revenue; strongest case studies |
| 4 | **Process Sprint** | 2–4 weeks, one process, baseline → fix → measured result | Delivery work |
| 5 | **Automation / AI build** | Quote after a sprint or workshop | Highest value; only after the process is stable |

The **advanced audit** is not a separate offer on the main page. Offer it inside the scorecard report
as "the next step if you want a deeper look".

## 3. Pricing recommendation

### Recommendation: show prices, use a launch price, add a guarantee
Reasons:
- Most Hungarian training and consulting firms hide prices. A visible price makes you look confident
  and makes the decision easier for a small company owner.
- You have no client references yet. A **launch price for the first 5 partner companies**, in
  exchange for permission to publish the results, is an honest reason for a lower price. It also
  creates the case studies you need. It does not look like a discount from someone who is desperate.
- A **guarantee** removes the risk that a new name brings. Example: "If the workshop does not find at
  least {{DECIDE: e.g. 10 hours}} of monthly saving, you do not pay."

### Market reference point
An open (public) 4-day Lean Quality course by Kvalikon/EOQ MNB in Budapest cost 295,000 Ft + ÁFA per
person in 2024 (about 74,000 Ft per person per day).
Source: https://eoq.hu/2024/mh241014l.pdf
In-company training for a group usually costs more per day in total, but less per person.

### Suggested price list (my estimate — test it in 3–5 sales talks before you publish)
| Offer | Regular price | Launch price (first 5 partners) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Process discovery workshop (½ day) | 190,000 Ft | 120,000 Ft — credited if you continue |
| 1-day team training (max. 12 people) + 30-day review | 490,000 Ft | 320,000 Ft |
| Improvement programme (3 days + projects) | from 1,390,000 Ft | from 890,000 Ft |
| Process Sprint (2–4 weeks) | from 990,000 Ft | from 690,000 Ft |
| Automation / AI build | quote | quote |

Notes:
- Travel outside {{FILL: home city}} is extra, or include it up to a distance you choose.
- **ÁFA (VAT):** in 2026 you can choose subjective VAT exemption (alanyi adómentesség) if your
  annual revenue stays under 20 million Ft (22 million Ft in 2027). Then write "Az árak alanyi
  adómentesek (AAM)." If you do not choose it, write "Az árak + ÁFA." Ask your accountant which is
  better: most business clients can reclaim VAT, so VAT does not make you more expensive for them.
  Source: https://nav.gov.hu/ado/afa/Emelkedik_az_alanyi_adomentesseg_ertekhatara
- **Training registration:** selling competence-development training as a business probably requires
  registration as an adult training provider (felnőttképző). See `07-legal-checklist.md` before you
  sell the first training.
