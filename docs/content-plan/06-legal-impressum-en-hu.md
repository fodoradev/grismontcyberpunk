# 06 — Impressum (HU + EN)

Pages: `/hu/impressum` and `/impressum`.
Legal basis: Ektv. (2001. évi CVIII. törvény) 4. § — the website must show the provider's name,
address, contact data (including email), registration number, tax number and the hosting provider's
address and contact data (including email).
Source: https://www.mnb.hu/letoltes/7-kozos-fogy-ved-rendelkezesek-v1.docx (summary of Ektv. 4. §)

**Rules for implementation**
